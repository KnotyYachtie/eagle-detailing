# Principles (non-negotiable)

1. **Luxury is subtraction**  
   If the fix is “add another layer,” first ask what can be **removed**. Restraint beats decoration.

2. **One brand, three domains**  
   Marine · Aviation · Automotive share **one visual system** — not three micro-aesthetics. Parity in nav, cards, and copy weight matters more than any single hero photo.

3. **Surface leads, chrome follows**  
   Photography carries authority; UI exists to **frame** and **read**, not to compete with the image.

4. **Precision over hype**  
   Language and layout signal **discipline** (short lines, structured lists, no loud stat walls). Proof is photographic and real — not implied by widgets.

5. **Motion is atmosphere, not entertainment**  
   Easing is long and calm; choreography overlaps deliberately. Nothing bouncy, nothing that reads as “app delight.”

6. **Mobile browser chrome is a first-class layout input**  
   Treat the OS bar as **part of the composition**: align what you *can* paint (`theme-color`, page canvas, hero bleed, optional pre-scroll). Never pretend the seam does not exist.

7. **Brand field vs neutral field**  
   **`#102135` remains the brand ground** for product UI. A **neutral near-black canvas** on the homepage is allowed **only** to stop Safari from re-tinting chrome with navy — not to replace the brand story elsewhere.

8. **Accessibility and calm coexist**  
   `prefers-reduced-motion` **opts out** of surprises (auto-scroll, extra transform overrides). Focus rings stay visible and on-accent.

9. **When a band won’t go away, own it**  
   If a horizontal strip persists (trust row, browser UI), **name it as design** (letterbox, cinema bar) instead of fighting it with weak tints.

10. **Glass is a tool, not an identity**  
    Translucency exists to **separate layers** at interaction boundaries; it must **degrade** to solid surfaces where blur is unsupported — never ship “broken glass.”
